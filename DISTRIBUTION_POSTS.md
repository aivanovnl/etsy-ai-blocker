**Title:** Made a free extension that hides the obvious AI-mass-produced "12,000+ patterns bundle" listings from Etsy search

Made a small Chrome extension that scores each listing (huge item counts, "whole shop/lifetime access" wording, price that doesn't add up for the claimed quantity, known repeat-offender shops) and hides the obvious ones, with a "show anyway" if it gets one wrong.

**Title:** AI Filter for Etsy — hides likely AI-mass-produced listings from Etsy search (open source)

**Body:**
Built this after noticing Etsy search results are increasingly dominated by mass-produced "mega bundle" digital-download listings crowding out real handmade sellers. It scores each search result on several signals (absurd item counts, "whole shop/lifetime access" wording, price-per-item math, a community-maintained shop blocklist) rather than a single keyword match, and shows you exactly why something was hidden.

No accounts, no analytics, fully local — settings never leave your device. Source + the blocklist (which auto-updates daily) are public: https://github.com/aivanovnl/etsy-ai-blocker
